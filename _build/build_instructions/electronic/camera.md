# USB Camera

Camera used for alignment. E.g. USB Camera Module OV9726 CMOS 1MP Camera Module. 

Note that the camera is required for both 1S-1F and 2S-14F models. In the former, the camera connects directly to the host PC. In the latter, the camera connects to a hub on the main board and thus indirectly to the host PC.

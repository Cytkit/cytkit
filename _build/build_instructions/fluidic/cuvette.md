# Cuvette / thick-walled quartz capillary

This is a very important component as it is the interface between the laser beam, the emitted light, the cells and the sheath flow.

The well-known supplier of quality cuvettes for flow cytometry is Hellma, from which individual cuvettes can be sourced according to the specification below (no standard part number). Inner and outer square sections are best for FSC/SSC performance.

Alternatively, much cheaper thick-walled quartz capillaries can be sourced from various suppliers. These should also be 4 mm outer dimension (circular or square) and 0.2 mm inner dimension (circular).

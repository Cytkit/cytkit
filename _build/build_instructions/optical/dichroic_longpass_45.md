# Dichroic longpass filter at 45 degrees (Beamsplitter)

This filter splits off the majority of the laser light at 488 nm towards the SSC detector. The filter should be substantially transparent above 500 nm to avoid cutting off blue/green-emitting fluorophores such as GFP, FITC and BB515.

500nm, 25.2 x 35.6mm, Dichroic Longpass Filter  (45 Degrees)

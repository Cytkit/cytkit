# Cover (with optics diagram)

Encloses the optics and prevents exposure to the beam, interfacing mechanically with the interlock switches. Contains holes for adjustment (fine alignment). Contains the optical diagram and labels of optical components for adjustment.

Note: first two layers (top two in the completed part) may be printed in a bright colour to allow contrast to read the optical diagram.

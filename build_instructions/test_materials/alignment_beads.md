# Alignment beads

Bright fluorescent beads for instrument alignment are sold by many flow cytometry instrument manufacturers. But you may also obtain them from suppliers of generic microparticles for research, often more cheaply. Make sure they are sensitive to the 488 nm laser and fluoresce strongly in the green-yellow region of the spectrum.

Beads should be made of polystyrene and a preferably large (around 10 um) for easy scatter alignment too.

# Rainbow beads (6-um, 6-peak)

The Spherotech 6 peak rainbow beads are commonly used in flow cytometry to check the sensitivity, precision and dynamic range of the fluorescence channels. 

The 6-um PS particles have higher scatter (FSC and SSC) than the more commonly used 3-um particles.
